The five-vendor problem

Most founders who want all of this end up here the same way.

  1. 1

    The checklist

    You know what you need: a company, a growth system, content that doesn't stop, a website, maybe a voice agent, and someone to handle replies. The checklist is clear.

  2. 2

    The vendors

    A setup agent for the company. An agency for outbound. A freelancer for content. A developer for the site. A recruiter for the hire. Five contracts, five timelines, five people who don't talk to each other.

  3. 3

    The gaps

    Each piece works on its own. Nothing is built to connect. The website doesn't feed the outbound system. The content doesn't match the voice agent's script. You become the integration layer, and you didn't sign up to be one.

That's the problem SFC Complete solves. One team builds every piece so they're designed to work together from day one, not bolted on after the fact.

FAQ

Can I swap one of the builds for something else, like SEO & AI Visibility or the DM Lead Engine instead of AI & Outreach Consulting?

Yes. Talk to us on the strategy call. We'll substitute one in-house build of equal value for another; the bundle price stays the same.

Do I have to take every piece?

SFC Complete is priced as a full bundle. If you only want one or two pieces, our individual builds and the lighter Launch + Grow or Launch + Scale packages are built for that.

How long does the whole build take?

Most clients are fully live, company, growth system, content, website, voice agent, and setter, within 60 to 90 days, since everything is built in parallel rather than one piece at a time.

Who pays the setter or VA?

You do, directly. Our fee covers sourcing, vetting, trial, placement, and support. Most setters and VAs charge $5 to $20 an hour depending on experience and skillset; typical base pay runs $500 to $1,000 a month.

Does property investment come with this package?

No. We keep that decision separate from your build, since timing a property purchase shouldn't be rushed to fit a package. You still get a free property and Golden Visa consult with our licensed partner, same as every other package.

Will moving my company to the UAE stop me paying tax at home?

Not automatically. US citizens are taxed on worldwide income wherever they live, and UK rules depend on your personal residency and where the company is managed and controlled. Speak to a qualified tax adviser in your home country before you move. Our partners handle the UAE side; we do not give tax advice.

What is your refund policy?

If you're not happy with the work we deliver in-house, tell us within 10 days of starting and we'll refund our fee for that service. After 10 days, our fee isn't refundable. Partner-delivered services, like the company setup and tax registration, follow our partner's own refund terms, since they invoice and deliver that work directly.
