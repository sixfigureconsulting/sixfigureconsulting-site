Almost every founder we speak to goes through the same three stages before they move.

  1. 1

    You see founders you respect setting up in Dubai. The tax numbers look almost too good. You start reading.

  2. 2

Sound familiar? The problem is rarely the decision. It's the coordination: structure, license, visa, bank, and tax, all run by different people who don't talk to each other.

What waiting is actually costing you

Not the cost of setting up. The cost of not doing it, or doing it badly.

You're a UK founder paying the additional rate

Income above £125,140 is taxed at 45%, and dividends at 39.35%. If you're relocating personally, every year of delay is another year at those rates. (Your home-country position needs its own advice.)

Your quotes don't compare

Setup quotes are built differently: license only, license plus desk, or everything except the extras. The line items left out are the ones that surprise you after you've paid.

You miss the tax election

Small Business Relief lets companies under AED 3M revenue elect nil taxable income, but it isn't automatic. You have to register and elect it on your return. Plenty of new companies don't.

Your new company has no plan to sell

A license doesn't bring clients. Founders who move without a go-to-market plan spend their first months paying UAE costs with no local pipeline.

FAQ

How long does setup take?

It depends on the free zone. Some issue the license within days once your documents are complete; visa and banking steps take longer. Your setup partner confirms the exact timeline for your zone once they have your documents, as part of your setup and tax snapshot call.

Do I have to live in the UAE?

No. You can own and run a free zone company from abroad. A residence visa is only needed if you want to live there.

Will moving my company to the UAE stop me paying tax at home?

Not automatically. US citizens are taxed on worldwide income wherever they live, and UK rules depend on your personal residency and where the company is managed and controlled. Speak to a qualified tax adviser in your home country before you move. Our partners handle the UAE side; we do not give tax advice.

Why pay $500 when your setup referral is free?

Referral-only setup gets you the license. Launch adds corporate tax registration support, a full GTM Blueprint, and our coordination across every step. If you only want the license, use the UAE Business Setup page.

Is Small Business Relief guaranteed?

No. It depends on your revenue and structure, and qualifying free zone persons are excluded. Our tax partner confirms whether it applies to you.

What is your refund policy?

If you're not happy with the work we deliver in-house, tell us within 10 days of starting and we'll refund our fee for that service. After 10 days, our fee isn't refundable. Partner-delivered services, like the company setup and tax registration, follow our partner's own refund terms, since they invoice and deliver that work directly.
