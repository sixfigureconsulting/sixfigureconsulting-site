The developer-or-DIY trap

You know you need a real website. Here's how the story usually goes for a founder trying to get one live:

  1. 1

    The Quote Shock

    You ask a developer or agency for a quote. $3,000, $10,000, sometimes more — plus weeks of back-and-forth before anything's actually live.

  2. 2

    The DIY Detour

    You try to build it yourself on a page builder instead. It takes longer than expected, the design never looks quite right, and you're paying a monthly subscription for a site that still doesn't feel like yours.

  3. 3

    The Stall

    Weeks turn into months. The business is operating without a real website, or with one that doesn't reflect where it's actually at. Every day without a site that converts is a day of missed credibility and missed leads.

Does that sound familiar? You don't need a $10,000 developer or a DIY platform you fight with every time you want to change a headline. You need a system that builds it once, properly, and lets you run it yourself afterward.

What inaction is actually costing you

This is the part most founders don't calculate. Not the cost of getting a website built — the cost of operating without one, or with one you can't touch.

A developer quote eats months of runway before a single page is live

Custom development typically runs $3,000–$15,000+ and weeks of revision back-and-forth, before you've earned a single dollar from the site.

DIY builders cost less upfront, but you pay in recurring fees and your own time

Monthly subscription builders add up every year, and building it yourself means hours you're not spending on the business.

No website, or an outdated one, costs you credibility with every visitor

Prospects check your website before they take you seriously. A missing or dated site is a silent objection you never actually hear.

Updating a developer-built site means emailing someone and waiting

Every small content change becomes a ticket, an invoice, or a delay. A site you can't update yourself is a site that goes stale.

FAQ

What if I need more than 10 pages?

Apply and tell us what you're building — we'll scope a custom package for larger sites. The 10-page standard package covers the vast majority of founder and small business websites.

Do I need to know how to code to make updates?

No. Once your site is live, you can ask for changes in plain language through a chat interface, and the update ships without you touching any code.

Who owns the website, hosting, and domain?

You do. The site lives in your own GitHub repository, hosted on your own Vercel account, with your domain routed through your own Cloudflare account. If you ever stop working with us, everything stays yours.

What's the difference between Website Build and Website Care?

Website Build is the one-time fee to design, build, and launch your site. Website Care is optional, billed yearly, and covers ongoing updates and maintenance if you'd rather we handle it than do it yourself via chat.

How long does it take to go live?

Most standard builds go live within 1–2 weeks of receiving your content and brand direction, depending on how quickly drafts are reviewed and approved.

Can you also handle a custom domain, email, or ecommerce?

Domain connection is included. Email and ecommerce can be scoped as part of a custom package — apply and tell us what you need.

What if I want to redesign later?

If you're on Website Care, request changes anytime via chat. If you're not, reach out and we'll scope a one-time update project.

Is this the same kind of site as sixfigureconsulting.co?

Yes — we build your site the same way we built and run this one: AI-designed and coded, hosted on Vercel, versioned in GitHub, and routed through Cloudflare.
