SEO, AEO, and GEO — what's actually different

Three acronyms, three different ways to actually get found. Here's what each one means before we talk about what we build.

SEO — Search Engine Optimization

Ranking in Google's traditional top 10 results for searches your buyers already run. The original game, still relevant.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Being the answer Google features directly — the AI Overview, the featured snippet — not just another blue link on the page.

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

Being the source AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini actually cite or recommend when someone asks a question in your space.

Here's why this matters now: more buyers are asking AI tools directly instead of searching Google first. If your content isn't structured and published consistently, you're invisible to all three — not just Google.

No. It's a one-time fee to build the keyword research, content automation, and publishing system. You own it afterward and run it with minimal involvement — we're not billing you monthly to manage it.

Will you guarantee we rank #1 or get cited by ChatGPT?

No, and anyone who promises that isn't being straight with you. We build the system correctly — proper keyword targeting, answer-first content structure, consistent publishing — which is what actually drives rankings and AI citations over time. Do I need a WordPress site?

No. We can publish to WordPress, a newsletter platform like Beehiiv, or another CMS you're already using. Tell us what you have when you apply.

Minimal. The system drafts and schedules content automatically — you or your in-house SEO person does a periodic review and approval pass, not daily management.

What's the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO is ranking in Google's traditional results. AEO is being the answer Google features directly, like a featured snippet or AI Overview. GEO is being the source AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity actually cite when someone asks a question in your space. We build content structured to work across all three, not just one.

Do we own the system?

Yes. It's built in your own Make and CMS accounts. If you ever stop working with us, you keep the full system.
