What an AI voice agent actually is

Before anything else, here's what you're actually buying — not a buzzword, a specific piece of technology with a specific job.

This is not the phone tree you're picturing. An AI voice agent isn't a menu of “press 1 for sales.” It's a real-time conversation: the caller talks normally, the AI understands what's said, and it responds in natural speech — built specifically around your business, not a generic script.
  1. 1

    A call connects

    Someone calls your business, or the agent dials out to a number on your own list.

  2. 2

    Speech becomes text

    What's said is converted to text in real time, so the AI can actually understand the conversation, not just detect keywords.

  3. 3

    The AI decides what to say

    Using your business information, call scripts, and guardrails, the AI works out the right response — the same way a trained rep would.

  4. 4

    Text becomes natural speech

    The response is converted back into natural-sounding voice in real time, so the call flows like a conversation, not a robot reading a script.

Before we build anything, here's what's required

This isn't fine print we hope you skip. These are the two non-negotiable rules behind every outbound agent we build.

Your list only — never cold calling. We build outbound agents to call your own list: people who've inquired, booked, bought, or otherwise have an existing relationship with your business. FAQ

Can you call a cold list we bought or scraped?

No. We only build outbound agents for your own list — people who've inquired, booked, bought, or otherwise have an existing relationship with your business. This is a compliance requirement, not a preference.

Does the AI tell people they're talking to an AI?

Yes. Every agent we build discloses that it's an AI at the start of the call, by design. We don't build agents that pretend to be human.

What's the difference between the build fee and usage costs?

The $2,500 is a one-time fee to design, build, and test your agent. Usage costs — call minutes, AI model usage, telephony — are billed separately and directly by Vapi or Retell AI and ElevenLabs, based on how much the agent is actually used.

How much do usage costs typically run?

It depends entirely on your call volume and average call length, and platform pricing changes over time, so we won't quote a number here. We'll walk you through estimated usage costs for your specific use case before you commit to a build.

What if I need something more complex than a standard support or outbound agent?

We'll connect you with a partner from our network who specializes in complex, multi-system voice AI builds. We don't have an in-house team for that scope — the standard build is what we build ourselves.

Is this a cold-calling service?

No. We don't do cold calling and we don't work with cold-call lists. This is a tool for your own customer support, or for calling people who already have a relationship with your business.

Who owns the voice agent after it's built?

You do. It's built on your own Vapi or Retell AI and ElevenLabs accounts, not ours.

How long does it take to build?

Most standard builds go live within 1–2 weeks of receiving your call scripts, business information, and any integration details.
