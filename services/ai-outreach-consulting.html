The referral ceiling

You've built something real. Your pipeline just hasn't caught up yet. Here's how the story usually goes for a B2B agency, consultancy, or service business:

  1. 1

    Year 1–2 — The Grind

    You grind. You close your first few clients through your network. Word spreads. Referrals start coming in. Revenue climbs. You think — this is working.

  2. 2

    Year 3–4 — The Plateau

    You're at $200K, maybe $500K. Most of it is still referral-driven. You've tried a few things — LinkedIn posts, maybe a cold email campaign from a freelancer — nothing stuck. You tell yourself you'll figure out outbound “when things slow down a bit.”

  3. 3

    Year 4+ — The Ceiling

    Things never slow down. But growth plateaued. The pipeline looks full until it doesn't. One big client leaves, and you feel it immediately. You're not building a business anymore — you're maintaining one.

Does that sound familiar? The referral model is not a business model. It's a starting point. Every founder who has been in the service business for a couple of years had to make one decision at some point: stop waiting for referrals, and build a predictable outbound system that books qualified sales conversations on demand.

What inaction is actually costing you

A US-based SDR costs $62,000–$90,000 in year one — before tools, training, management overhead, and a 3–6 month ramp. And if they leave? You start again.

Here's what makes it worse: most of the founders we speak to have been meaning to “sort out outbound” for 12–18 months. They know they need it. They've just been waiting for the right moment, the right hire, or the right agency. The right moment never comes on its own — you have to build it.

FAQ

How long until we see results?

First qualified replies typically appear in weeks 2–3. Consistent meeting flow — 8–17 per month — is usually established by month 2–3. We do not promise a specific number of meetings. We promise the system is built correctly and optimised every week until benchmarks are hit.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the system: built correctly, monitored daily, and fixed within 7 days if any metric drops below benchmark. We do not promise a specific number because outbound performance depends on variables that differ by client — offer quality, ICP size, deal complexity. What we can tell you is that every client we've worked with who had a qualified offer and realistic ICP hit their meeting targets by month 3.

What is the minimum commitment?

Retainers run month-to-month after the first 60 days. We ask for 60 days because it takes 30 to build properly and 30 to optimise — cancelling before that means we have not had time to make it work. After 60 days, cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

We tried cold email before and it did not work.

This is the most common thing we hear. Cold email campaigns fail for predictable reasons: shared or new domains, generic copy, no signal logic, no funnel for replies. What you had was not a system — it was a campaign. Apply and tell us what you ran. We will tell you exactly why it did not work and what we would do differently.

Do we own the infrastructure at the end?

Yes. Everything is built in your accounts — Make/n8n, Instantly, HeyReach, GHL. We configure, manage, and optimise, but you own all of it. If you ever stop working with us, you keep the full system.

How is this different from other outbound agencies?

Most agencies run campaigns on their own infrastructure. You rent access — and lose everything when you cancel. We build in your accounts, use signal-based targeting instead of static lists, and monitor performance daily instead of sending a monthly report. The methodology, the tooling, and the ownership model are all different.
