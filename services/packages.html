FAQ

Why don't you show the partner's price?

Because only they can quote it accurately. License, visa, and government costs depend on your exact free zone, visa count, and activity, and our partner is the licensed party for that side of things, not us. They quote and invoice you directly, with no markup from us. The price you see on this page is only our fee, for the work we do and the coordination across everyone involved.

Do I pay the partner separately?

Yes. Our setup partner invoices their own fees directly, and we invoice ours separately. You still deal with one point of contact the whole way through.

Can I just get the business setup, without a package?

Yes. Use our UAE Business Setup page. Referral-only setup costs you nothing extra, because the partner pays us. Packages are for founders who also want tax registration, a growth plan, or an outbound system handled.

Can I start small?

Yes. The Agent Setup Blueprint is $197, and its credit applies if you upgrade within 30 days.

What is your refund policy?

If you're not happy with the work we deliver in-house, tell us within 10 days of starting and we'll refund our fee for that service. After 10 days, our fee isn't refundable. Partner-delivered services, like UAE company setup or tax registration, follow our partner's own refund terms, since they invoice and deliver that work directly.

Not sure which package fits?

Apply and tell us where you're starting from. We'll recommend one package, or none if it isn't a fit.
