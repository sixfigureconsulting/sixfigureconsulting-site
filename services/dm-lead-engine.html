Who this is built for

This build only works if your team can actually send and follow up. Before you look at pricing, be honest with yourself about whether this describes your situation.

This is for you if:

  • You have a team that can send DMs and follow up: setters, SDRs, AEs or a VA
  • Ads or cold lists bring you leads that don't reply or don't fit
  • Your buyers are on Instagram or LinkedIn: founders, consultants, coaches, investors, business owners
  • You have a clear offer and know who it's for
  • You want to own the system, not rent an agency forever
  • You're happy to spend a small monthly amount on data tools, from about $29 a month

This is not for you if:

Why your outreach isn't getting replies

Most teams land here the same way, through the same three steps.

  1. 1

    The list

    You buy or scrape a big list. Most of it is outdated, private, fake, or the wrong person entirely.

  2. 2

    The message

    You send the same template to everyone on it. Buyers spot a template in about two seconds.

  3. 3

    The silence

    Hundreds of DMs go out. Replies come back close to zero. Your team burns out, and you're back to paying for ads.

The problem isn't your team or your offer. It's who you're messaging, and what you open with.

We hit this exact wall ourselves. Hundreds of messages, no replies. So we rebuilt how we pick leads and how we open the conversation. Now we get replies. This package builds that same system for you.

The cost of doing nothing

This is what a broken list and a generic opener are already costing you every month, whether you're tracking it or not.

Wasted setter hours

Your team spends hours a week sending and following up on leads that were never going to reply. That's paid time spent on a list problem, not a sales problem.

Paid ads that don't convert

Signal-based leads cost a fraction of what you're paying per lead on ads, because you're reaching people already showing interest instead of paying to interrupt strangers.

No learning loop

What we build

A daily lead engine that runs on its own every morning and drops ready-to-send leads into one shared sheet.

Signal sourcing

We map the pages your buyers already follow: hubs, communities, events, competitors, and niche creators. The engine pulls the newest followers of those pages every day.

Qualification and research

Every new profile gets checked. Company pages, competitors, agents, students, and inactive accounts are removed. The real fits get researched: bio, business, recent posts, current activity.

Ready-to-send openers

Each qualified lead gets three first messages in different tones: Curious, Casual, and Direct. Each one references something real from the profile. Your setter picks one, copies it, and sends.

You own everything: the sheet, the hub list, the accounts and the tool logins. If we stop working together tomorrow, the engine keeps running.

FAQ

How many leads will I get a day?

It depends on your budget and how many signal pages we can verify for your market. Most Starter-level budgets land in the tens of ready leads a day, not hundreds. We'll give you a realistic number on the strategy call once we've seen your niche.

Do you send the DMs for me?

No. Your team sends and follows up from your own accounts. We build the engine that finds, qualifies, researches and drafts the opener. Sending stays with your setters, which is also what keeps your accounts safe.

Will my Instagram accounts get banned?

Not if you stay within the daily limits we set and avoid mass templated blasting. We help you set up and warm up your sender accounts and set a safe daily budget as part of the build.

Can you do cold Facebook outreach?

No. Facebook outreach in this build only covers groups you're already a member of and people already in your network. Cold messaging strangers on Facebook isn't part of this package.

Do you create LinkedIn accounts?

No. You connect your own LinkedIn account or accounts to HeyReach, and we build the targeting and the messaging on top of it.

What does my team need to do?

Budget roughly 1 to 2 hours a day for every 50 leads in the sheet, for sending, following up and logging replies. We'll fine-tune this estimate with you on the strategy call.

Is this legal? What data do you use?

We work only with public profile information. You're responsible for how you store and use that data under rules like GDPR in your market. This isn't legal advice, so check with your own counsel if you're unsure.

What happens after the support period?

The engine keeps running. You can adjust the filters and signal pages yourself, or add an optional monthly tuning add-on if you'd rather we keep optimizing it for you.

Ready for leads worth messaging?

Tell us who you sell to. We'll show you where your buyers are already raising their hands.
