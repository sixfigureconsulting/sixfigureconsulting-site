Frequently Asked Questions

Is Six Figure Consulting a licensed tax, legal, or real estate firm?

No. For UAE business setup, tax, real estate, and USA business funding, we connect you with licensed, vetted partners who do the regulated work — we don't provide legal, immigration, financial, tax, or lending advice ourselves. Our in-house team handles AI outreach, AI content consulting, and talent placement directly, since that's our core expertise.

How does Six Figure Consulting make money?

Honestly: for anything delivered through our partner network, the partner pays us a referral fee or commission for the introduction — it doesn't cost you anything extra to go through us. For our in-house services (AI & Outreach Consulting, AI & Content Consulting, and Talent Placement), you work with and pay us directly.

Why do you partner with other businesses instead of doing everything yourselves?

Because AI-driven growth systems are what we're actually experts in — not law, tax, or real estate. Rather than pretend otherwise, we built relationships with specialists we trust, so you get one point of contact instead of managing five vendors yourself. It's also how we can service the wider founder community well, instead of a handful of clients badly.

Does using Six Figure Consulting cost me more than going direct?

No. Connecting with a partner through us costs you nothing extra — our fee comes from the partner, not from you.

What if I only want the AI services, not the partner network?

That's fine — apply for exactly what you need. AI & Outreach Consulting, AI & Content Consulting, and Talent Placement are all delivered in-house, directly by us.
