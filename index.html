Frequently Asked Questions

Is Six Figure Consulting a licensed tax, legal, or real estate firm?

No. For UAE business setup, tax, real estate, and USA business funding, we connect you with licensed, vetted partners who do the regulated work — we don't provide legal, immigration, financial, tax, or lending advice ourselves. Our in-house team handles AI outreach, investor outreach, AI content consulting, and talent placement directly, since that's our core expertise.

How does Six Figure Consulting make money?

Honestly: for anything delivered through our partner network, the partner pays us a referral fee or commission for the introduction — it doesn't cost you anything extra to go through us. For our in-house services (AI & Outreach Consulting, Investor Outreach, AI & Content Consulting, and Talent Placement), you work with and pay us directly. Our combined packages mix both: you pay us one fixed fee for the in-house work and coordination, and, if the package includes UAE company setup, our partner invoices you separately and directly for their own costs.

Why do you partner with other businesses instead of doing everything yourselves?

Because AI-driven growth systems are what we're actually experts in — not law, tax, or real estate. Rather than pretend otherwise, we built relationships with specialists we trust, so you get one point of contact instead of managing five vendors yourself. It's also how we can service the wider founder community well, instead of a handful of clients badly.

Does using Six Figure Consulting cost me more than going direct?

For a referral-only introduction, no: our fee comes from the partner, not from you. Our combined packages are different: alongside the partner's own costs, you pay us a separate, fixed fee for the in-house work and coordination we add.

What if I only want the AI services, not the partner network?

That's fine — apply for exactly what you need. AI & Outreach Consulting, Investor Outreach, AI & Content Consulting, and Talent Placement are all delivered in-house, directly by us.
